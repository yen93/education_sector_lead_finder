Education Sector Lead Finder

Finding elite K-12 schools and education associations every day

A daily automation that discovers independent K-12 schools and education associations in Australia, the US and the UK and saves them as leads. It aims for about ten brand-new organisations per run, skips anything already on file, and copies the new ones into a Google Sheet for the team.

What it does

  • Searches Google Maps through Apify, working through a grid of regions and search terms, oldest-searched first.
  • Keeps only elite school-type organisations: excludes universities, vocational colleges, business schools and similar, then requires a school-type category or strong name, then a minimum number of reviews.
  • Associations skip the category and review checks.
  • Checks against both the manually found leads and the auto-found leads, so nothing is added twice.
  • Stops once it has found its target of new organisations or reaches its time limit.
  • Works at organisation level only; no contact enrichment.

How it's built

Architecture

  • A Supabase scheduled job calls one edge function every day at 06:00 Philippines time.
  • The function loads its settings and the region grid, scrapes, filters, saves, mirrors to the Sheet and writes an audit row.
  • It runs in the background and is time-boxed so it always finishes before the platform's time limit.
  • Tuning (target count, review floor, keywords, categories, grid cells) is plain SQL on settings tables; no redeploy needed.

Tech stack

  • Supabase edge function (Deno / TypeScript) and Postgres
  • Supabase scheduled job (pg_cron + pg_net)
  • Apify Google Maps scraper actor
  • Google Sheets API via a service account
  • Tables: the lead output, a run audit log, the discovery grid and a one-row settings table

Things to know

  • Google Maps has no student numbers, so "large school" is approximated by search terms, category filter and review count; the enrollment column stays empty.
  • On small regions the time limit can yield fewer than ten new organisations.
  • Peak bodies without an association keyword in their name are classed as schools.
  • Nothing is ever deleted: duplicates are skipped and retired grid cells are disabled.
  • The Sheet write is by column position; if columns are added or reordered, the function's row mapping must be updated.
  • Ten early Melbourne rows pre-date the tuning and include some vocational and business colleges.
